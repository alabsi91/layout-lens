Questions we get asked

Does it work with no signal?

Yes. Notes are stored on the device and sync later on their own when you come back into range. Nothing waits on a connection.

Can the crew write in the same log?

On the Offshore plan, yes. Everyone aboard writes into one log and their entries merge in order once a phone finds signal.

Where do the tide tables come from?

National hydrographic data, downloaded for the region you are sailing before you leave, so it is on the phone when you need it.

Can I get my notes out again?

Any time, on either plan. Export the whole log as PDF or plain Markdown files, one per entry, with the positions intact.
