The harbour works

Work began in the spring of 1908 and stopped twice, once for money and once for weather. The east arm was laid first because the shipping office wanted the coal berth usable before the winter, and the contractor agreed to it against his own advice.

What survives today is mostly the second attempt. The original blocks were cast on the beach and floated out at high water, a method the county surveyor called cheap and the harbour master called reckless. Both were right.
