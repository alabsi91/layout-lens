Signing

Every delivery carries a signature header built from the raw body and your endpoint secret. Compare it with a constant time check.

Secrets rotate on request and both keys stay valid for an hour.

Retries

A delivery that does not answer with a 2xx inside ten seconds is retried eight times with an exponential backoff.

After the last attempt the endpoint is paused and you get an email.

Paused endpoints keep their queue for 24 hours.

Event types

Invoices, payments, ledger closes and account changes each publish their own event, and the payload always carries the full object.

New fields can appear without notice, new event types cannot.
